# Article short description (for Builder Center)

**Title:** Weekend Showcase Challenge: Infinite Mashup Studio Motion 2.0

**Cover image:** `article-assets/motion-2-cover.png`

**Short description:**

I submitted Infinite Mashup Studio to the Creative App Challenge — fuse ingredients into a painted invention, a dossier written from the image, and a spoken origin story. It worked, but the invention stayed frozen on screen. Motion 2.0 is what came next: the same fuse pipeline, plus story-driven Nova Reel video and an original AI score. Longer origin stories become longer motion reels. The still stays; the lore moves. Built on AWS Amplify, Lambda, Bedrock (Nova Pro, Nova Lite, Nova Reel), S3, DynamoDB, Polly, and Cognito — inspired by @Lewis Sawe's Museum That Grows.

**One-liner (if character-limited):**

Fuse impossible inventions into stills + stories, then animate the origin into a motion reel with Nova Reel — Motion 2.0, the evolution of Infinite Mashup Studio.
